About us

GoodGridCo is a small studio in Italy that makes printable puzzles and activities built on grids: word searches, sudoku, mazes, mystery games and more. We design for real people: big, bold letters, levels that truly differ, and an answer key for everything. Our own software creates every puzzle and checks it before it goes in the shop.

GoodGridCo Publisher

GoodGridCo Publisher is our internal tool. It runs on our own computer and uploads the short videos we make about our products to our own YouTube channel, TikTok account, Instagram account and Facebook Page. It is not offered to other people or businesses, and it does not access anyone else's account.

Every video it uploads is labelled as made with AI tools, as each platform requires.
